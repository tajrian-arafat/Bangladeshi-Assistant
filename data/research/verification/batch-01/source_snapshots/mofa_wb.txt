Issuance of original certificate or copy of certificate after correction of information in both Bengali and English. free of charge US$: 01/- 7. Issuance of copy of original certificate both in Bengali and English TK: 50/- US$: 01/- List of documents required for Birth Registration: A). The following documents should be attached with the application submitted within 45 (forty-five) days of the person's birth: (i) Proof of Place and Date of Birth, clearance certificate from the concerned medical institution or attested copy of birth certificate obtained from the said institution or birth attendant's certificate or any other certificate: And (ii) Birth Registration certificate or National Identity card or passport or any other proof of the person's permanent address; or Up-to-date copy of proof of payment of tax in domicile declared as permanent address of the person being registered or parents or Grand parents; And Birth Registration Certificate or National Identity card of the registered person's parents. B). In case of submission of application submitted after 45 (forty-five) days of the person's birth should be accompanied by the following in addition to the above-mentioned documents: (i) Duly filled in application form attested by EPI personal or a copy of EPI card, if applying within 05 (five) years of birth; (ii) Certificate issued by a competent doctor to proof date of birth if applied after 5 (five) years of birth; or (iii) Educational Certificate of Primary Education Completion, Junior School Certificate and Secondary School Certificate or similar examination conducted by the Education Boards of Bangladesh.
